FAQ

Common questions

What does design cost?A design consultation is $250, waived with a furniture purchase of $5,000 or more. Full-service interior decorating is ideal for projects from about $10,000. Each project gets one fixed proposal before anything is ordered.+
Do I have to buy from Capital Discount Furniture?Most projects do, because that is what makes one quote and one delivery possible. Pieces you already own are worked into the plan.+
Can I just come to the showroom?Yes. Ask for Brooklyn or Alexa at 3921 S. Miami Blvd. in Durham.+
How long does a project take?Showroom stock installs quickly; manufacturer orders set the timeline. Both are written into the proposal.+
Which areas do you serve?Durham, Raleigh, Chapel Hill, Cary, Apex, Wake Forest and nearby.+
