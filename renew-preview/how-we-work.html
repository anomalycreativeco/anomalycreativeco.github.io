The process

Five steps

Your dates and your price are fixed in step two.

Step 01

Tell us about it

A few questions about the property, the timeline and the goal. Photos help.

Step 02

Walkthrough & proposal

We see the space, in person or from photos, and send a fixed proposal with an install date.

Step 03

Design & pull

Your designer plans each room and pulls every piece from our warehouse.

Step 04

Deliver & install

Our crew delivers, places and styles. Most homes are finished in a day.

Step 05

Sold, kept or cleared

When the home sells we clear it out, or the buyer keeps what they love.
