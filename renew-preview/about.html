Our story

Born from the Triangle’s highest-rated furniture store

Founders’ dates and quotes to confirm with Scott and Jenny
Our story video · the Chapel Ridge home, shortened from the 8-minute Prewitt-Douglas cutVideo 16:9▶

Capital Discount Furniture has been family-owned in Durham since 2013. Scott and Jenny Danielson built it into the Triangle’s highest-rated furniture store, with a 50,000 square foot showroom and warehouse, twenty-plus manufacturer lines and a delivery crew of its own.

Builders and agents kept asking the showroom to furnish homes that had to sell. The designers, the inventory and the trucks were already there, so staging became its own company: Renew Staging & Design.

Renew still runs on that infrastructure. Every project is designed in-house, pulled from inventory Renew owns rather than rents, and installed by the same crew that loads the truck. When a buyer or homeowner falls for the furniture, they can keep it.
