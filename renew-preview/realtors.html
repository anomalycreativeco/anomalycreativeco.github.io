Why staging matters

Buyers decide from the photos

Your listing gets a few seconds in a feed of other listings. Staging is what makes those seconds count, and it reflects on you as much as on the house.

Empty rooms photograph small

Without furniture there is nothing to show scale. A staged room tells buyers where the sofa goes and that the king bed fits.

The showing starts online

Buyers book showings from the photos. Rooms that look finished and cared for get opened; blank ones get scrolled past.

Sellers judge the prep

How the home is presented is the part of your service the seller can see. Staging makes the listing appointment promise visible.

The Full Process
  1. Step 01

    Send the address

    Tell us the list date, which rooms matter and whether the home is vacant.

  2. Step 02

    Quote & install date

    A fixed proposal, usually from photos, with the install date in writing.

  3. Step 03

    Design & pull

    Your designer plans each room and pulls every piece from our warehouse.

  4. Step 04

    Install & style

    Our crew delivers, places and styles. You book the photographer for the next day.

  5. Step 05

    Under contract

    We clear the home around your closing date, or the buyer keeps what they love.
