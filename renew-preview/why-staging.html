Why staging matters

What buyers actually do in a home

Furniture is not decoration. It changes how a buyer reads the house.

Fifteen seconds

Buyers spend about six minutes in a home and form an opinion in the first fifteen seconds. A furnished room wins that moment.

They stay longer

Buyers walk through a vacant home in minutes and linger in a furnished one. The longer they stay, the more likely the offer.

Faults get forgiven

Almost a third of buyers (28%) will overlook property faults when the home is staged.

Common questions

“Can’t buyers just imagine it?”

Some can. Most cannot, and you are selling to most.

Isn’t staging expensive?Staging a vacant home usually costs less than one mortgage payment. The average seller discounts 8% after 78 days on market; a stager typically costs 1.5% or less.+
Isn’t virtual staging enough?It helps the photos but not the showing. Buyers walk into the empty room the photos hid.+
Is it worth it on a lower-priced home?Staging can be scoped to the rooms that matter: living, kitchen, primary bedroom.+
What if the home is occupied?We work with the seller’s furniture and fill the gaps.+
