The brief

Two communities, one staging partner

Scope: full-service staging, designed, delivered and installed by Renew. New Home Inc. builds in Parker’s Landing, Zebulon, and Duncan’s Creek, Lillington. Each model needed to photograph and show as a home, ready on the builder’s schedule.

Renew designed each home to its finishes, pulled the furniture from its own inventory and installed with its own crew. Several models staged across the two communities; each one below shows different rooms.

Buyers can keep it: one buyer liked the staged pieces enough to purchase the sectional from the model, and has since furnished more of the home through Capital Discount Furniture. That is a resource no other stager can offer a builder’s buyers.
