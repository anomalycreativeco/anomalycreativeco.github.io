Why furnish?

A furnished model sells the plan

Buyers walk a model to picture the life, not the lot. Furniture is what turns square footage into rooms.

Scale is hard to read

An empty great room looks either huge or awkward. Furniture shows where the sectional and the ten-seat table go.

The model is your showroom

It is the one home every buyer in the community sees. It should look like the brochure.

Spec homes compete with resale

A staged spec photographs like a lived-in listing. Garman Homes’ Knightdale model took a full-price offer seven days after Renew staged it.
