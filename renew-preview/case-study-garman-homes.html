The brief

Several homes in the community, one staging partner

Scope: full-service staging, designed, delivered and installed by Renew. Garman Homes builds in Wendell and The Preserve at Marks Creek, east of Raleigh. Each finished home needs to photograph and show as a home buyers can picture living in, on the builder’s date.

Renew designs each home to its finishes, pulls the furniture from its own inventory and installs with its own crew. The first home, 2873 Mar Bonita Street, took a full-price offer on October 22, seven days after staging. The second, 1244 Ponder Lake Pkwy in Wendell, was staged in November.
