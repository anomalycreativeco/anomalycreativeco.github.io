The brief

A few hard-to-find pieces, on a deadline

Scope: partial furniture sourcing. The house was complete and the client’s own designer had the interiors in hand. What was missing were a few statement pieces nobody could get in time.

Midtown asked Renew to source them: a custom plum sectional for the basement speakeasy, and outdoor furniture for the green porch, the pool deck and the side courtyard.

Renew found and delivered them on the client’s schedule. Photos and wording on this page are subject to the client’s approval.
