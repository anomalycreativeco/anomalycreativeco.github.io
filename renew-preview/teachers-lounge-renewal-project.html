How it works

From nomination to reveal

The same team, warehouse and crew that stage homes for builders and agents, pointed at a school.

Step 01

Nominate

A teacher, parent, PTA or neighbor tells us about the lounge and sends a few photos.

Step 02

We visit

We walk the space with the school, measure it and talk through what the staff actually needs.

Step 03

Design & furnish

Renew designs the room and pulls seating, tables, lighting and accessories from inventory and partner lines.

Step 04

Reveal

Our crew delivers and installs in a day. The teachers walk in. We film it.

Why we do this

It started with our kids’ schools

The first two lounges were personal: schools our own kids attend. Jenny has a teaching degree, and as parents we know how much teachers give every day. A comfortable, welcoming room to recharge in is a small thing we can actually do.

Between Capital Discount Furniture and Renew we have the inventory, the designers, the delivery team and the vendor relationships to do this well, without the cost that usually comes with a project like it.

The community impact stays front and center. Anything that comes back to the two businesses comes from doing something meaningful and doing it well.
