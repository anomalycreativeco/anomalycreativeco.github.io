The brief

Their design, our furniture

Scope: Furnishing only. Prewitt-Douglas is a design-build company, so their own designers wanted to stage the home. Renew was happy to work alongside them to choose and source the perfect furnishings that achieved their design vision.

The home had distinctive and high-end finishings: marble waterfall island, custom wood paneling, vaulted and beamed ceilings. What it needed was designer furnishings that fit the elevated spaces.

Renew helped select and recommend items that their team loved, ordered it to their precise timing for a Parade of Homes reveal, delivered it and set it with its own crew, and left the final touches to their talented designers.
