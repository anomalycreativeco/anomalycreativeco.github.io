The brief

A finished custom home that needed to read as a home

Scope: full-service staging, designed, delivered and installed by Renew. 1725 Legacy Ridge Lane was complete and move-in ready: stone fireplaces, a vaulted dining room, a covered porch with its own hearth. Empty, those rooms photographed as volume, not as living space.

Exeter needed the home staged for listing photography and for private showings during the Parade of Homes weekends, on a fixed date, without coordinating a separate design, rental and moving vendor.

Renew designed to the home’s palette of stone, walnut and brass, pulled the furniture from its own inventory and installed with its own crew ahead of the photo date.
